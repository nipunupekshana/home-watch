# home-watch privacy policy

home-watch is a personal tool used only by its owner. It has no other users.

Google data: home-watch only accesses files that it creates itself in the owner's Google Drive (Google scope drive.file). It cannot see, read or change any other files in the Drive.

What it stores: short video clips from the owner's own home cameras, saved in a "Home cameras" folder in the owner's Drive. Clips are deleted automatically after 21 days.

Sharing: no data is sold, shared or used for advertising or analytics.

Control: the owner can remove home-watch's access at any time in their Google Account, under Security, Third-party connections.

Last updated: 14 November 2026.
